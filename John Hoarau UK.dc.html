CFD of a four-stage axial turbomachine · ESTIA

Mar 2026

Compressible, turbulent, non-isothermal flow in ANSYS CFX 2024 R2: RANS with SST k-ω and total energy, 1.83 million tetrahedral elements, air at −40 °C and 1,000 km/h around blades held at 450 °C. The run did not reach a steady solution; a gas-dynamics analysis traced this to an ill-posed velocity inlet (static inlet Mach 0.993, choked inter-blade passage) rather than a numerical fault, and a corrected boundary-condition set was proposed. A thermo-elastic pre-analysis (free thermal growth 0.26 mm, 1.47 GPa if restrained) justified a one-way FSI approach and a root fixing that leaves the blade free to expand.
